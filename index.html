1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how this application (ads-api, "the Application") handles data obtained through the Amazon Advertising API and the Amazon Selling Partner API.

The Application is an internal tool operated by a single independent seller for managing its own advertising campaigns and marketplace operations. It is not offered to the public, is not distributed as a commercial product, and is not used to provide services to any third party.

2. Whose data is accessed

The Application accesses only the advertising account and seller account owned by its operator. It does not access, request, or store data belonging to any other advertiser, seller, or Amazon customer.

3. What is not collected

The Application does not request restricted roles and does not receive personally identifiable information about Amazon customers. It does not collect or process buyer names, shipping addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, or payment details. It does not build profiles of individuals and does not engage in behavioural targeting or retargeting.

4. What the Application processes

Only business data related to the operator's own account:

  • Advertising configuration: campaign names, ad group structure, keywords, bids, budgets, and entity states
  • Advertising performance: impressions, clicks, cost, attributed sales, ACOS and ROAS
  • Search term reports used for negative keyword management
  • Marketplace operational data obtained through the Selling Partner API: orders, inventory levels, settlement and fee reports, and listing information

5. How the data is used

Solely to operate and improve the operator's own business: bid and budget adjustments, negative keyword management, campaign performance analysis, per-product profitability calculation, and inventory replenishment planning. No data is used for any other purpose.

6. Storage and security

  • Data is stored on infrastructure controlled and administered solely by the operator
  • Encryption in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher; encryption at rest for stored data
  • Access is limited to a single administrator account protected by multi-factor authentication
  • API credentials and refresh tokens are held in encrypted runtime configuration and are never committed to source control
  • Access logs are retained and reviewed periodically

7. Sharing and disclosure

Data is not sold, rented, traded, published, or shared with any third party. There are no advertising networks, analytics providers, data brokers, or external data processors involved. Data is not used to train third-party models and is not transferred outside the operator's own infrastructure.

8. Retention and deletion

Advertising performance data is retained for up to 24 months for trend analysis. Financial and settlement records are retained for up to 24 months for accounting and audit purposes. Data beyond these periods is deleted automatically by scheduled jobs.

9. Cookies and tracking

The Application is a server-side integration. This website does not set cookies, does not run analytics scripts, and does not track visitors.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in data handling practices. The revision date at the top of this page indicates the most recent update.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy, about data handling practices, or requests regarding data deletion can be directed to 1453246715@qq.com. This is also the email address registered to the Amazon Developer account that owns the Application. Requests are answered within five business days.
